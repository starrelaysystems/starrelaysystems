The problem isn't your team. It's the tools they're stuck with.

Most small businesses we talk to are running leads through a mix of WhatsApp, memory, and a spreadsheet someone stopped updating months ago. Nothing is lost on purpose — it just falls through a system that was never built to hold it.

Enterprise CRMs solve this but charge per seat, which turns hiring into a tax. We build the same core discipline — a pipeline, roles, and follow-up tracking — priced flat per business, so your bill doesn't change the day you bring on a fourth agent.
